Turn a raw 3D animation into a film.

A cinematic pass, as a skill, built on the look we finish our own films with: it paints and lights your scene, shoots it through a lens, moves the camera and tells you where the sound goes.

What the skill adds

look

Our house look

A warm room with one cool line: our palette and light balance as a starting point, or the same structure in your colours. Nothing in the frame stays grey.

light

Light

A key, a rim, lamps that really light the room, soft shadows and haze. And a light of its own for any camera that needs one, as on a set.

camera

Lens and camera

Focus that falls away, motion blur, a faint streak off the lamps. Moves with a reason: a slow push, a focus pull, a snap onto a face.

sound

A sound map

Where every sound goes: on the cut, the move, the action. Mixed from your own sound files, at the loudness phones expect.

It also carries the twelve rules we light and shoot by, written so Claude applies them to your scene and checks every shot against them. Your animation is never changed, so the skill can show you both at once, before above and after below, in one command.

What is not in it

This is one pass of our process, not all of it. It finishes an animation you already have; it does not write a story or build a scene. Our set, our characters, their voices and our sound library stay with us, and the skill brings no sound files of its own.

What you need

  • Claude Code
  • An animation of your own made with three.js, or the sample scene that comes with the skill
  • Node.js 20 or newer, ffmpeg and Google Chrome
