Privacy notice

What we keep, and why.

Last changed 2026-11-12.

Who we are

NōTI is run by Wiktor Begier and Juliusz Dukaczewski (Poland), responsible for how your address is used. Write to contact@withnoti.com about anything on this page.

What we keep

  • Your email address, when you ask for the skill.
  • When you signed up and when you confirmed, as proof that you asked.
  • Whether a mail reached you, and whether it was opened or a link in it was clicked. The mailing service records this for every list it sends for.

This site itself sets no cookies and loads nothing from other companies. The fonts and the video come from this site.

Why

To send you the skill you asked for, and to send you NōTI's mails about making videos with AI. We do this because you asked us to (your consent, Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR). You can take it back at any time: every mail has a link that takes you off the list with one click, or write to us.

Who else handles it

Kit sends our mail and keeps the list for us, in the United States, under a data processing agreement with us. Nobody else gets your address. We do not sell it and we do not use it for advertising elsewhere.

How long

Until you leave the list. After that we keep only a note that your address must not be mailed again.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, have its use limited, get a copy, and object to its use. Write to contact@withnoti.com. If you think we got it wrong, you can complain to your data protection authority; in Poland that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl).
